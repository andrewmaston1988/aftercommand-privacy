AFTERCOMMAND — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026.

AFTERCOMMAND (com.aftercommand.game) is a strategy game for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your data: nothing leaves your device.

What the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not request permission to access the internet, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data on your device

Anything the game keeps, such as settings, stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about the download and install under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
